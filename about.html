About me

From clinical data to a working, explainable system.

I work across the full path from idea to working system: preparing clinical and tabular data, designing and evaluating models, explaining them with SHAP and LIME, and shipping web applications in Spring Boot, Flask and Django.

My research focuses on interpretable, privacy-aware machine learning for healthcare. I enjoy work where a model has to be right, understandable to a clinician, and careful with the data it was trained on.
